BEFORE THE LESSON

Your Word is a lamp to my feet and a light unto my path.
— Psalms 119.105

Read the Scripture lesson for the evening.

Take time for silent reflection, then say:

May it be unto me according to your Word.
— Luke 1.38

  or

My soul magnifies the Lord
  and my spirit rejoices in God my saviour,
  for he has regarded the low estate of his handmaiden.
For behold, hereafter all generations will call me blessed;
  for he who is mighty has done great things for me,
  and holy is his name.
And his mercy is on those who fear him
  from generation to generation.
